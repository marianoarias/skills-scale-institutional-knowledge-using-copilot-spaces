# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Project Sponsor / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors own strategic sponsorship, remove organizational barriers, confirm priority and major trade-offs, and approve significant scope or investment decisions. They ensure alignment with business strategy and remove impediments that block team progress.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and major scope changes
- Confirm priority and resolve strategic trade-offs
- Remove organizational barriers and secure resources
- Provide executive visibility and stakeholder alignment
- Approve significant investment or risk decisions

### Goals
- Ensure project delivers business value and strategic alignment
- Remove blockers that inhibit team progress
- Maintain executive visibility and stakeholder confidence

### Typical Communication
- Project charter approval and decision gates
- Escalation resolution and resource requests
- Status briefings at decision milestones
- Stakeholder alignment and risk acceptance

### Decision Boundaries
- Approves project scope, timeline, and investment
- Makes final decisions on trade-offs between business priorities
- Resolves conflicts between stakeholders or teams
- Accepts business-level risks

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager:** Confirms business outcomes and priority alignment
- **Project Manager:** Receives escalations and approves major changes
- **Stakeholders:** Represents their interests at decision gates

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Decision Boundaries
- Own implementation approach and code quality decisions
- Estimate work effort in collaboration with technical leads
- Identify and escalate technical risks

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Technical Lead / Architect:** Aligns on technical direction and design patterns
- **QA/Test Lead:** Collaborates on acceptance criteria and test coverage
- **DevOps/SRE:** Works with on deployment and operational readiness
- **Product Manager:** Clarifies requirements and acceptance criteria

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Decision Boundaries
- Define success metrics and measure outcomes
- Prioritize backlog and roadmap
- Make trade-off decisions between features and scope
- Accept product-level risks

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Owner:** Partners on backlog refinement and acceptance
- **UX/UI Designer:** Collaborates on problem definition and user research
- **Developers:** Clarifies requirements and success measures
- **Data/Analytics Partner:** Defines measurement and outcome tracking
- **Customer Support / Operations:** Incorporates feedback on customer impact

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Decision Boundaries
- Manage project schedule and milestone tracking
- Identify and escalate risks and dependencies
- Coordinate resource allocation within team constraints
- Facilitate decisions on timeline and scope trade-offs

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Project Sponsor:** Escalates blockers and major decisions
- **Product Manager:** Aligns on roadmap and release planning
- **Technical Lead:** Coordinates on estimates, dependencies, and technical risks
- **All Team Members:** Facilitates planning, standups, and retrospectives

## Product Owner

### Role Summary
Product Owners translate product strategy into an actionable backlog, clarify requirements and acceptance criteria, and make day-to-day prioritization decisions. They act as the bridge between product vision and development execution.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain a prioritized, well-refined backlog with clear acceptance criteria
- Clarify requirements and answer questions from the development team
- Make day-to-day prioritization and scope decisions
- Collaborate on Definition of Done and quality standards
- Accept completed work and validate against acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Enable the development team to work efficiently with clear, actionable work items
- Ensure delivered features meet acceptance criteria and user needs
- Maintain team velocity and predictability

### Typical Communication
- Backlog refinement sessions with the team
- Clarification of requirements during implementation
- Acceptance and sign-off on completed work
- Daily or on-demand availability for questions

### Decision Boundaries
- Prioritizes work within a sprint or iteration
- Clarifies and adjusts requirements based on team feedback
- Accepts work as meeting acceptance criteria
- Makes trade-off decisions on scope within an iteration

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager:** Receives prioritized roadmap and feedback from field
- **Developers:** Clarifies requirements and accepts completed work
- **QA/Test Lead:** Collaborates on acceptance criteria and test cases
- **Technical Lead:** Discusses feasibility and trade-offs

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads own technical direction, architecture decisions, integration design, and technical risk management. They guide the team on design patterns, scalability, and system reliability.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate technical direction and architecture
- Make or guide major architecture and design decisions
- Review design proposals and ensure consistency with architecture
- Identify and manage technical risks
- Support developers with design guidance and mentoring
- Coordinate with DevOps/SRE on operability and deployment

### Goals
- Deliver architecturally sound, scalable, and maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and complexity
- Ensure system reliability and operational readiness

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Guidance on implementation approaches
- Technical risk documentation
- Coordination with DevOps/SRE on deployment readiness

### Decision Boundaries
- Makes or guides architecture and major design decisions
- Approves significant technical trade-offs
- Identifies and escalates technical risks
- Defines technical standards and patterns

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Guides on design and implementation approaches
- **Project Manager:** Communicates estimates and technical dependencies
- **Security/Privacy Partner:** Incorporates security requirements into design
- **DevOps/SRE:** Ensures system is operationally ready
- **QA/Test Lead:** Collaborates on test strategy and coverage

## UX/UI Designer or User Researcher

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers represent user needs through research, interaction design, journeys, and usability validation. They ensure solutions are intuitive, accessible, and meet user needs.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather user insights
- Design user journeys and interaction flows
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate designs through usability testing
- Ensure accessibility and inclusive design
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria from a user perspective

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, usable, and accessible solutions
- Understand and represent user needs and behaviors
- Validate that solutions meet user expectations
- Reduce post-launch support and user confusion

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and insights
- Design specs and interaction flows
- Usability test results and recommendations
- Collaboration with product and development teams on design trade-offs

### Decision Boundaries
- Defines user needs and experience requirements
- Makes interaction and design decisions based on user research
- Validates designs meet usability and accessibility standards
- Recommends prioritization based on user impact

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager:** Collaborates on problem definition and success measures
- **Developers:** Ensures design is implementable and provides guidance on UX edge cases
- **QA/Test Lead:** Collaborates on usability test cases and acceptance criteria

## QA/Test Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Test Leads define the test strategy, coordinate validation, track quality risks, and confirm readiness against acceptance criteria and the Definition of Done. They ensure quality standards are met and identify issues early.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and test plan for features
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria and Definition of Done
- Coordinate test execution (manual, automated, exploratory)
- Track quality risks and defect trends
- Validate features meet acceptance criteria before release
- Identify process improvements to reduce defects

### Goals
- Ensure delivered features meet quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Identify and prevent defects early in the development cycle
- Build confidence in release readiness

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and test case documentation
- Quality dashboards and defect tracking
- Acceptance validation and sign-off
- Quality-related feedback in retrospectives

### Decision Boundaries
- Approves test coverage and test strategy
- Determines readiness for release based on quality metrics
- Prioritizes defect fixes based on severity and impact
- Makes trade-off decisions on manual vs. automated testing

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Partners on test coverage and defect resolution
- **Product Owner:** Collaborates on acceptance criteria and acceptance testing
- **Technical Lead:** Discusses test strategy and coverage for complex components
- **DevOps/SRE:** Coordinates smoke tests and production validation

## DevOps/SRE or Release Manager

### Role Summary
DevOps/SRE or Release Managers own deployment automation, environment readiness, observability, release coordination, and rollback preparedness. They enable reliable, predictable releases and operational excellence.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and automation
- Manage deployment environments (staging, production)
- Coordinate and execute releases
- Monitor system health, performance, and errors
- Prepare and execute rollback procedures
- Ensure security scanning and compliance in deployment

### Goals
- Enable frequent, reliable, low-risk releases
- Maintain system uptime and performance
- Reduce deployment cycle time and rollback time

### Typical Communication
- Release notes and deployment coordination
- Observability dashboards and incident alerts
- Deployment runbooks and procedures
- Collaboration on incident response and post-mortems

### Decision Boundaries
- Determines deployment readiness based on infrastructure and monitoring
- Approves release timing and deployment procedures
- Makes decisions on rollback and incident response
- Defines deployment and operational standards

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Developers:** Ensures code is deployable and monitors production behavior
- **Technical Lead:** Aligns on operability requirements and deployment architecture
- **QA/Test Lead:** Coordinates smoke tests and production validation
- **Project Manager:** Communicates deployment timeline and risks

## Security/Privacy Partner

### Role Summary
Security/Privacy Partners advise on threat modeling, security controls, privacy requirements, and security review gates. They embed security and privacy considerations into design and implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct threat modeling and risk assessment
- Define security and privacy requirements
- Review design and implementation for security risks
- Advise on compliance and regulatory requirements
- Participate in security testing and scanning
- Support incident response and post-mortems

### Goals
- Deliver secure, privacy-compliant solutions
- Reduce security vulnerabilities and privacy risks
- Ensure compliance with regulations and standards

### Typical Communication
- Security requirements and threat models
- Security review findings and recommendations
- Compliance documentation
- Incident response and post-mortem participation

### Decision Boundaries
- Identifies and escalates security and privacy risks
- Approves security controls and mitigation strategies
- Makes recommendations on compliance and regulatory requirements
- May block release if critical security issues exist

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Technical Lead / Architect:** Collaborates on secure design and controls
- **Developers:** Advises on secure implementation practices
- **DevOps/SRE:** Aligns on security scanning and monitoring
- **Project Manager:** Escalates security risks and compliance issues

## Data/Analytics Partner

### Role Summary
Data/Analytics Partners define instrumentation, reporting, and measurement plans so success metrics can be evaluated after release. They enable data-driven decisions and outcome measurement.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and measurement approach
- Design telemetry and instrumentation
- Build dashboards and reporting
- Analyze data to validate outcomes
- Identify insights and optimization opportunities
- Collaborate on data privacy and ethical considerations

### Goals
- Enable measurement of project outcomes and success
- Provide actionable insights for continuous improvement
- Support data-driven decision making

### Typical Communication
- Measurement plans and instrumentation specs
- Dashboards and reporting
- Analysis and insights documentation
- Outcome reviews and retrospectives

### Decision Boundaries
- Defines what metrics to measure and how
- Recommends prioritization based on data and insights
- Advises on data privacy and ethical considerations

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager:** Defines success metrics and interprets outcomes
- **Developers:** Collaborates on telemetry instrumentation
- **Project Manager:** Provides data for milestone and progress reporting
- **DevOps/SRE:** Ensures logging and monitoring infrastructure supports measurement

## Customer Support / Operations Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Operations Representatives bring support, service, training, and operational-readiness needs into planning and release activities. They represent the voice of the customer and support teams.

### Responsibilities
- Identify support, training, and operational readiness needs
- Participate in planning to anticipate support impact
- Collaborate on release communications and training
- Gather feedback from customers and support teams
- Identify common issues and improvement opportunities
- Participate in incident response and support during launches

### Goals
- Ensure customers and support teams are ready for release
- Reduce post-launch support burden
- Improve customer experience and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Support readiness checklists and training plans
- Customer feedback and common issues
- Release communication drafts
- Post-launch support observations and recommendations

### Decision Boundaries
- Identifies support and operational risks
- Recommends training and communication approach
- Escalates customer impact issues

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Product Manager:** Shares customer feedback and impact
- **Project Manager:** Communicates on release timing and communications
- **DevOps/SRE / Release Manager:** Coordinates support readiness and rollout
- **Developers:** Clarifies edge cases and known issues for support preparation

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Refer to decision boundaries and interactions to understand how personas collaborate and where handoffs occur.
- Use cross-persona interactions to identify communication patterns and dependency points in your project processes.
